The problem with a shortlist PDF

A CV attached to an email gets about six seconds. Then the same questions come back to you: "Tell me more about her stakeholder work." "Why did he leave X?" "Has she done this in banking?" Every round trip is a delay between your shortlist and a placement — and your candidate reads the silence as rejection.

What your client gets

  • Ask the profile questions in plain language and get answers drawn from the candidate's own verified input — not guesswork.
  • Minutes of real engagement per candidate instead of a six-second skim.
  • Better-informed interviews, fewer mismatched ones, faster yes or no.

What your agency gets

  • A shortlist your client has never seen from a competitor.
  • Fewer clarification round trips per role.
  • A candidate experience that reflects well on you — candidates keep the profile for their own search.

What it takes — honestly

This is not a bulk CV upload. Each profile is built from the candidate's CV plus roughly 30 minutes of guided input from the candidate — structured questions about what they actually did and owned. The AI does the assembly; the candidate approves the content; your consultant reviews before anything reaches a client. Good profiles take a little effort. That is exactly why they work.
